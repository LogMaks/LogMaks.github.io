_In publication process_   


The article introduces a hierarchical multi-agent reinforcement learning (HRL) approach for optimizing maintenance and repair planning (MRO) in industrial enterprises.
Unlike predictive maintenance, which forecasts failures, the proposed method focuses on decision-making and co-optimization of repair actions under resource constraints and uncertainty.

The model integrates Dec-POMDP ↔ SMDP/CMDP formulations, combining hierarchical RL, CTDE, and action masks with Lagrangian constraints.
At the top level, the system performs strategic planning (window allocation, resource balancing); at the lower level — tactical dispatching of repair crews in real time.

Experiments in a digital twin environment show the method’s potential to reduce downtime, SLA delays, and operational costs, while ensuring safety and compliance through masked actions and constrained optimization.
This framework lays the foundation for adaptive, intelligent maintenance systems in the Industry 5.0 paradigm.
