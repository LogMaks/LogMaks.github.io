About Me

I’m Maksim Loginov, an AI researcher and developer with a strong focus on Reinforcement Learning (RL), Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), and intelligent decision-making. My research explores how hierarchical and safe RL architectures can be applied to optimize complex industrial processes — particularly in predictive maintenance, reliability analysis, and automation. I have hands-on experience in Python, C++, SQL, and TensorFlow, as well as a growing interest in MLOps, simulation environments, and digital twins. Currently, I’m completing my Master’s degree and preparing for Ph.D. studies, where I plan to continue developing adaptive, multi-agent control systems for real-world applications. Beyond research, I enjoy building practical prototypes, visual dashboards, and simulation tools — transforming theory into working systems. My long-term goal is to contribute to innovative AI research that bridges academic knowledge and industrial impact. Also, I'm fully open to scientific collaborations in RL fields.
